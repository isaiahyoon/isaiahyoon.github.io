Pricing

Website launches start at $1,000.

After a consultation, we’ll agree on the scope and price before work begins. Your quote will specify the pages, features, and revision rounds included. More complex features, such as online booking, e-commerce, or custom integrations, can increase the project price.

Payment schedule

50% to begin. 50% before launch. After we sign the agreement, half is paid before work begins. The rest is paid after you approve a working website preview, before launch. For a $1,000 project, that’s $500 upfront and $500 before launch.

Payment accepted through Venmo, Cash App, Zelle, or cash. Payment details are provided after we agree on the project.

After launch & ongoing costs

Updates and additional work after launch are $100 per hour. We’ll discuss the work and estimated cost before proceeding. Corrections to meet the agreed requirements are included at no extra charge.

Domain, hosting, and third-party services may have separate costs, which we’ll discuss in advance. For simple websites, hosting can be $0/month, depending on the provider and plan limits.
